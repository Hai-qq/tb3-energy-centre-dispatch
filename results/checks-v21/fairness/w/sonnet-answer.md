1. Per app/data/supply.json, Saturday 8 Aug 2026, 03:00 (weekend table, 00:00-07:00 band): import 5.0 p/kWh, export 5.9 p/kWh. Tuesday 21 Jul 2026, 03:00 (weekday table, same band): import 13.8 p/kWh, export 5.9 p/kWh. Export is flat all day. Caution: app/planner/tariff.py takes the weekday from the UTC timestamp of the first half-hour. In summer local midnight is 23:00Z the day before, so the tool would price Saturday as a Friday (13.8 p, not 5.0 p) and Monday as a Sunday. Tuesday is unaffected.

2. Not stated, and not enforced. Physically one net flow crosses the connection per half-hour (the docstring in app/dispatch.py lists one "grid exchange"), so only one should be positive. But app/planner/optimize.py makes import and export separate non-negative variables. They are linked only by the balance (import minus export equals net need) and capped by the 1220 kVA and 250 kW limits. On weekdays import costs 13.8 p or more against 5.9 p export, so the optimizer never uses both. In the weekend 00:00-07:00 band import (5.0 p) is cheaper than export (5.9 p). There it would import and export together, up to the 250 kW export limit. That is a paper gain of 0.9 p per kWh looped, which no meter would record and which inflates import_kwh and export_kwh.

3. Open: whether the exchange is net or gross. Alternatives: (a) net, at most one of import_kw and export_kw positive (a binary, or separate import-only and export-only LPs); (b) gross, separately registered, so the loop is allowed. instruction.md's "as they will be metered and billed" favours (a) but does not say so. "Weekend" is also undefined (Saturday and Sunday assumed).
